vLLM achieves bitwise-consistent RL training and inference

vLLM’s new post shows training and rollout producing bitwise-identical logprobs (diff = 0) on DeepSeek-V4-Flash, reaching 0 diff at 91.3% of the high-performance baseline’s rollout throughput.

The team carried the method to Nemotron-3.5-Lightning, where the consistent run’s SWE-bench Pro score held ~0.6 while the baseline fell from 0.56 to 0.22. The workflow is open-sourced as an agent skill plus recipes and code.

Why: Train–inference numeric mismatch turns on-policy RL into off-policy RL and can destabilize training—this is a practical, transferable fix squarely in the RL/inference-stack core.

ChinaTalk: the case for DUV controls

Saif Khan of the Center for Technology and Statecraft argues on ChinaTalk that stopping exports of DUV immersion lithography tools is the US’s most consequential lever over the AI balance.
