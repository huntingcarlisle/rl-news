Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on Nov 12 — the fast, cheaper third member of the Gemini 4 family, after Argon and Carbon — priced at $1.50/$7.50 per 1M input/output tokens, with a 1M-token context window and four thinking levels including a new xhigh tier.

Perplexity releases Decider v1.3 and Decider Flash v1.3, open-weight decision models that return probabilities

Perplexity released open-weight decision models on Nov 12 — a 27B model built on Qwen3.8-27B and a 4B distilled Flash — under Apache 2.0. They return probabilities instead of generated text, and the new Locate feature extracts verbatim text spans with character offsets for NER, PII detection and extractive QA.

Why: Perplexity reports 91.6% mean accuracy across 63 classification tasks — 6.54 points above its own production classifiers and ahead of TypeSafe's Jev 1.14 in finance — another strong entrant in the decision-model wave aimed at the classification layer inside agent pipelines, this one with downloadable weights.

DeepSeek published DeepInfer on GitHub under Apache 2.0 on Oct 20 — the inference engine it says runs its own production services, timed to day 1 of PyTorch Conf (a reversal: in April 2025 the lab said it would not release the full engine). Why: This is the first time a leading lab has open-sourced its production serving stack rather than just components — and it’s a fork lineage of vLLM/SGLang itself, so the playbook (persistent prefix caches, expert-parallel load balancing, adaptive speculative decoding, SWA replay) is directly importable competitive intel for the SGLang/vLLM inference ecosystem, plus a first-class Ascend 950 target that changes the hardware-portability conversation.
